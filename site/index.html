Our Commitment to Your Success

A team that stays with you

At Durable Life Skills, Inc., we are dedicated to transforming the disability workplace by fostering inclusion and providing pathways to rewarding careers. We believe in creating a supportive environment where individuals with disabilities can thrive.

Our passionate team works tirelessly to break down barriers and challenge the norms of the disability workforce. By partnering with you, we deliver personalized experiences that build real skills, real confidence, and progress that holds.

Common Questions

Answers about our inclusive services

Don't see your question? Ask us directly, we reply within one business day.

How does career coaching benefit individuals with disabilities?

Our coaching starts with each person's strengths and aligns career paths with their abilities. We provide a supportive environment to boost confidence and achieve meaningful employment.

What makes your training programs inclusive?

Our training integrates accessibility and personalization, ensuring every participant receives relevant skills in an inclusive setting. We focus on removing barriers to learning and career advancement.

How does job placement work for clients?

Job placement connects clients with inclusive employers that respect diverse skills. We guide them through every step, from application to employment, ensuring a harmonious fit and growth.

How are services funded?

Most participants access services through Colorado Medicaid waivers (such as DD, SLS, or CES), the Division of Vocational Rehabilitation, or private pay. We'll help you understand which applies to you during your consultation.
